AMRITA VISHWAVIDYAPEETHAM

Mata Amritanandamayi Devi (born Sudhamani Idamannel on September 27, 1953),
popularly known as Amma, the “hugging saint”, is a world-renowned
Indian spiritual leader and humanitarian from Kerala.
She is admired globally for spreading compassion and love
to reduce emotional and physical suffering.
